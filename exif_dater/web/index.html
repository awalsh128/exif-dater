Check known dates

    Prepare your photos (no credit needed)
    
      
      
    • Put only your scans in their own folder, and start the app with it. Analyzing sends every photo in
        the folder that isn't dated by name.
      • 
      
    • Start the names of photos you can date with the date, such as 1987 beach.jpg. They are
        read without using the API.
      • 
      
    • For the model test, add 20 or more dates you know under Model quality, then Check known dates. They
        are saved to truth.csv in that folder, which you can also write by hand with the header
        file,date.
      • 
    
    
  
    

  
    
    Make truth.csv from Google Photos
    
    This helps only with photos whose dates are right in Google Photos. A scan keeps its scan date unless
      you corrected it.
    
    
      
      
    1. At Google Takeout, export
        Google Photos, and unzip it into a new folder used only for testing.
      2. 
      
    2. In that folder, run this command. Change 2025-01-01 to the day you started scanning, so that scan
        dates are left out. It replaces any truth.csv there.
      3. 
    
    

    (echo file,date
exiftool -q -q -r -ext json \
  -if "\$PhotoTakenTimeTimestamp and \$PhotoTakenTimeTimestamp < $(date -d 2025-01-01 +%s)" \
  -if '-e "$Directory/$Title"' \
  -p '"$Directory/$Title",${PhotoTakenTimeTimestamp;$_=ConvertUnixTime($_);s/ .*//;tr/:/-/}' . |
  sed 's|^"\./|"|') > truth.csv
    
    
    
    Keep 20 to 50 rows of scanned prints, and shorten a date to the year if you guessed its month. Then test
      that folder: npx . evaluate <folder>. Don't analyze it, which would send every photo.
